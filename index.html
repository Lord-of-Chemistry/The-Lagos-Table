Our story

Born in Lagos.
Made for everyone.

The Lagos Table started in 2016 with one simple idea: Nigerian food deserves a seat at every great dining table.

From our kitchen in Victoria Island, we combine the bold flavours we grew up with and contemporary techniques to create food that feels both familiar and completely new.
